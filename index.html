About the conference

Bridging the divides in air quality science

Air Quality: Science for Solutions is an annual conference on air quality, with a focus on Utah and the Intermountain West. Its mission is to bridge the divides that separate researchers in different disciplines from each other, from professionals in the industrial and regulatory realms, and from the public, and to encourage a cross-pollination of ideas that leads to solutions.

It began in 2015 with a pair of conferences by the same name hosted by the Utah Department of Environmental Quality, Division of Air Quality; the Program for Air Quality, Health, and Society at the University of Utah; and the Utah State University Bingham Entrepreneurship & Energy Research Center. The format

What to expect

Topics

The subject of this conference is air science. While it focuses on issues important to Utah and the Intermountain West, we also welcome presentations on related air quality topics.

  • Atmospheric chemistry
  • Health impacts
  • Meteorology
  • Emissions
  • Modeling
  • Public policy

Oral sessions

There are no parallel sessions. Oral presentations should be understandable, at least in part, to air scientists from all fields. A major goal of this conference is the cross-pollination of ideas between fields of study.

Poster sessions

Posters are on display for the entire conference, and breaks are held near the posters to encourage interaction. There is also a student poster competition.
