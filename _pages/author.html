Call for Talks and Posters

The Air Quality: Science for Solutions conference seeks high-quality talks and posters on air quality issues important to Utah and the Intermountain West. We also welcome presentations on related air quality topics.

All abstracts are reviewed by the committee before being accepted. While we try to honor requests for oral or poster formats, only a small number of oral presentations are possible due to limited time.
