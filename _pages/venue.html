Venue Information

The conference will be held in the event space of BYU's Engineering Building, which is located on the southeast end of campus (map). There are a few options for getting to the Engineering Building.

Car

Visitors can park for free in lot 26V (map). If that lot is full, visitors can park in lot 2V (map). The map below shows both lots along with walking directions to the front of the building.

Public Transportation

The Engineering Building is a two-minute walk from the UVX BYU South Campus station. Take FrontRunner to Provo Station, then take the UVX bus toward BYU South Campus. Buses leave Provo Station every 6 minutes, and the ride takes about 18 minutes.
