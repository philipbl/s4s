Mission and history

The mission of this conference is to bridge the divides that separate researchers in different disciplines from each other, from professionals in the industrial and regulatory realms, and from the public, and to encourage a cross-pollination of ideas that leads to solutions.

Air Quality: Science for Solutions began in 2015 with a pair of conferences by the same name hosted by the Utah Department of Environmental Quality, Division of Air Quality; the Program for Air Quality, Health, and Society at the University of Utah; and the Utah State University Bingham Entrepreneurship & Energy Research Center.
