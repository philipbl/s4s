Presenter Guidelines

Oral Presentations

Talks will be 15 minutes maximum, with 3 minutes for Q&A and the transition to the next speaker, for a total of 18 minutes. The session moderator will step in to keep things on track if necessary. All talks need to be preloaded onto the conference computer before the conference begins.

Poster Presentations

Thank you for preparing a poster for the Science for Solutions conference. We look forward to viewing it and learning about your work. We want to archive your poster on the conference website and make it available to a wider audience, so in addition to bringing a printed copy to the conference, please upload a PDF of your poster using the link we will send you.

Posters will be displayed on easels like the one shown in the photo. Each easel holds a 42" × 36" poster board in either landscape or portrait orientation. We have found that landscape posters are generally easier to read, especially for people who use wheelchairs or are shorter in stature.
